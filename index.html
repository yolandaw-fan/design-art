    About

    Yolanda's artistic practice explores Chinese social and cultural heritage, drawing connections between her past and present experiences. Through her work, she reflects on her personal history, cultural identity, and the emotional significance of her ethnic background. Her projects invite viewers to immerse themselves in the richness of Chinese culture while gaining insight into her personal story and the multicultural experiences that continue to shape her artistic perspective.
